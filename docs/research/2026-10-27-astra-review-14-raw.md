1. **P1 — Export retains references after read permission is revoked.** [decisions.py:125](/home/mega/kobra/src/kutuphane/decisions.py:125). Citations and links use the earlier authorization snapshot; the final gate checks only exported journals. Reproduced by revoking access to referenced books from a second connection after `_records`: their passage citation and `derived_from` reference still appeared. **Fix:** use `guard_many`’s `finish` callback to filter references against the final read permissions.

2. **P1 — Export releases training text after a source restriction commits.** [decisions.py:85](/home/mega/kobra/src/kutuphane/decisions.py:85). Effective `ai_input` and `train` are captured once; the final gate never rechecks them. Reproduced with A → B → decision C: restricting A after `_records` returned still exported C’s text, options and choice, with both rights reported as `y`, although `note_journal` already returned false. **Fix:** recheck effective rights before delivery and redact affected records, or serialize export with restrictions through commit.

3. **P2 — Rollback labels a previously approved root “never accepted.”** [notes.py:829](/home/mega/kobra/src/kutuphane/notes.py:829). `ROOT_MOVE` considers only current Standing. Reproduced through supported operations: `approved → retired → pending → rollback` produces `rejected`, contradicting D68 and mislabelling the decision export. **Fix:** check the root’s approval history; a reopened, previously approved root must become `retired`. Reserve `rejected` for roots never approved.
